詠理 · Youri

A voice across distance

Youri is a 23-year-old musician and artist. She is not an idol and not a conventional hero. Her place in the world comes from the connections her music creates.

Her sound moves between languages and moods, carrying fragments of memory, isolation and earned hope. What reaches the listener matters more than a polished persona.

Before the music was heard

Still here

Long before anyone listened to Youri’s music, she learned what it meant to go unnoticed. Parts of her childhood were shaped by hunger, uncertainty and nights without a place that truly felt safe.

She wore clothes repaired from whatever she could find and braided coloured scraps into her hair—a small trace of identity in a life where almost nothing belonged to her. Music was not a career then. Sometimes it was simply a way to remain present: a melody under her breath, a rhythm against the noise of the city, proof that she had not disappeared.

Her life did not change in a single clean moment. There were brief encounters, people whose names she never learned and memories that would return years later in her songs.

Not every song describes who Youri is now. Some return to earlier versions of her; others hold feelings and stories that reach beyond her own life. Her music does not promise that pain disappears. It creates a place where it can be heard—and where, sometimes, someone answers.

Music as identity

More than a profession

Music is Youri’s language: the way she transforms what she cannot easily say and sends it beyond the boundaries of one place or one language.
